KYOTO BEN — PHOTO SWAP GUIDE

The website design is intentionally unchanged from your preferred V4.

FASTEST WAY TO CHANGE A PHOTO
1. Open assets/photos/
2. Replace the photo using the SAME filename.
3. Commit + push in GitHub Desktop.
That is all.

MORE FLEXIBLE WAY
Open assets/site-config.js.
Every image slot is named clearly. Change only the src filename.
You can also change position to adjust the crop without editing CSS.

Examples:
hero = homepage bamboo image
01-first-light-main / small
02-west-east-main / small
03-kimono-main / small
04-geisha-main / small
05-taiko-main / small
06-hidden-main / small
meet-ben = Meet Ben photo

To change crop:
position: "center center"
position: "center 35%"  (moves crop upward/downward)
position: "65% center"  (moves crop left/right)

Do not edit index.html just to swap photos.
