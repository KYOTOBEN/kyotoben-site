Meet Ben

Local context changes the way Kyoto feels.

I'm Ben, a Kyoto-based private guide who calls Arashiyama home, one of the most beautiful corners of the city. I live here with my family, my daughter, and our dog, surrounded by the mountains, temples and quiet streets that first made me fall in love with Kyoto. When I'm not guiding, you'll usually find me reading history, listening to traditional music, searching for something good to eat, or simply noticing the small details of everyday Kyoto life.
